Featured project · built for Muthoot Finance, 2026

Muthoot gold-loan voice assistant

After seeing CareFlow, an interviewer asked me to build a version for Muthoot Finance, India's largest gold-loan company, on Gnani's speech APIs. Customers get a loan estimate from their gold's weight and purity, book a valuation visit at a branch and check what they owe, by text or voice in English, Hindi, Kannada, Malayalam and Tamil. Every estimate that turns into a booked visit is tracked as a lead for the branch, which runs a live walk-in queue.

  • Cut time to first audio by 40%, from 7.4 s to 4.6 s at P50, by speaking a short first piece of each reply while the rest is synthesised.
  • Financial safety guardrails enforced in code: never asks for an OTP or PIN, never promises an amount, no investment advice, account numbers masked; 139 of 413 tests cover them in CI.
  • Built around a strict speech-API quota: one request at a time, retries, usage caps and replayed audio for repeated sentences, so voice falls back to text instead of failing.
  • Reliability: Gemini → Ollama → rule-based failover with circuit breakers; 1,134 requests from 50 simulated customers with 0 failures. An embedding classifier lifted intent accuracy on indirect phrasing from 12% to 68%.

Voice AI project, 2026

CareFlow: voice-first hospital assistant

Patients book appointments and follow a live queue; doctors run their queue and broadcast delays; admins see department load and the assistant's latency. CareBot routes each message to one of four specialist agents and answers by text or voice in English, Hindi or Kannada.

  • Two voice pipelines: Gemini Live speech-to-speech (first audio in 2.3 s at P50), and a cascade of Silero VAD, faster-whisper and streamed TTS, with barge-in and automatic fallback.
  • Halved the cascade's time to first audio, from 7.9 s to 4.0 s, by benchmarking each stage: a shorter Whisper window cut speech-to-text from 4.1 s to 1.0 s.
  • Safety guardrails block diagnoses, prescriptions and dosages, also on spoken replies, by checking the live transcript while holding audio for 300 ms.
  • Reliability: Gemini → Ollama → rule-based failover with a circuit breaker and a Redis response cache; 0 failures with 50 simulated patients at once.
